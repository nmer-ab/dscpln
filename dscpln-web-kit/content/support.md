# Support

We usually reply within 2 working days.

Email us at [**support@dscpln.app**](mailto:support@dscpln.app). Tell us your phone model and what happened.

### I don’t get my daily reminder

Check that notifications for dscpln are on in your phone settings, and that the reminder time is set in **Profile → Reminders**.

### Milo stopped answering

Ask Milo has a daily limit of 30 messages. It resets the next day.

### Where are my audio files?

Audio you add stays on your phone. If you delete the app or change phones, add the files again.

### How do I delete my account?

In the app: **Profile → Account → Delete account**. Or see [Delete account](/delete-account).

### Need urgent help?

dscpln is not a crisis service. If you’re struggling, contact local emergency services or find a helpline at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).
