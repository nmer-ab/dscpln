# Delete your account

You can delete your dscpln account and data at any time.

## In the app

1. Open dscpln.
2. Go to **Profile → Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

## By email

Send an email to [support@dscpln.app](mailto:support@dscpln.app?subject=Delete%20my%20account) with the subject **Delete my account**, from the email address you use in dscpln.

## What gets deleted

- Your account and profile.
- Your onboarding answers, saved lines, collections and streaks.
- Your Ask Milo messages.
- Your notification token.

We delete it within 30 days. Audio files are only on your phone; deleting the app removes them.
