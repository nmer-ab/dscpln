# Delete your account

You can delete your dscpln account and data at any time.

## In the app

1. Open dscpln.
2. Go to **Profile → My account**.
3. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

Want a copy first? In the same place, tap **Export my data**.

## By email

Send an email to [support@dscpln.app](mailto:support@dscpln.app?subject=Delete%20my%20account) with the subject **Delete my account**, from the email address you use in dscpln. We delete it within 30 days and tell you when it's done.

## Cancel your subscription too

Deleting your account **does not cancel your dscpln Pro subscription**. Apple or Google bill it, so cancel it there:

- **iPhone:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions → dscpln → Cancel subscription.
- **Android:** Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → dscpln → Cancel.

## What gets deleted

From our servers:

- Your account, email address and password.
- Your profile, name, language and onboarding answers.
- Your habits, the days you ticked them off, your streak and streak freezes.
- Your Milo chats.
- Your subscription status and creator code.

From your phone, when you delete in the app: your journal, mood, notes, saved lines, collections, history, audio you added and your settings.

Your data leaves our database right away and our backups within 30 days. Deleting the app alone removes what's on your phone, but not your account.
