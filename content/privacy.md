# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what the dscpln app and the dscpln.app website collect, why, and what you can do about it. We collect only what the app needs to work.

## Who we are

dscpln is made by Ahmed Ibrahim Abdallah, trading as TechLoops, Clermont-Ferrand, France. We are the data controller. Contact: [support@dscpln.app](mailto:support@dscpln.app).

## What we keep with your account

These are stored on our servers so they follow you to a new phone:

- **Account:** your email address, and your password if you set one (stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text). We use your email to sign you in, send your sign-in codes and answer you when you write to us.
- **Profile:** the name you give us, your language, your onboarding answers (your focus areas, habits, minutes a day, first streak, nudge times, theme and widget) and your reminder settings.
- **Habits and streaks:** the habits you pick, the days you tick them off, your streak, streak freezes and your time zone, so a day ends at your midnight.
- **Milo chats:** the messages you send to Milo, Milo's replies and how many messages you've sent today.
- **Subscription:** whether you have dscpln Pro, which plan and until when. We get this from Apple or Google through RevenueCat. We never see your card or payment details.
- **Creator code:** if you enter one, so the creator who sent you gets credit.
- **Reports:** if you report a fact or a line as wrong, what you reported.

## What stays on your phone

These never leave your phone and we can't see them:

- Your morning and evening journal, your mood, your notes and your breathing sessions.
- Your saved lines, favourites, collections, hidden lines and history.
- Audio you add to the app.
- Your widget's data, which the widget reads from the phone.

Deleting the app removes them. Deleting your account from inside the app removes them too.

## How AI is used

- **Milo:** when you write to Milo, your message and the recent conversation are sent to an AI model through OpenRouter, which passes it to the model provider to write the reply. We don't send your email.
- **Your daily lines:** to pick lines for you, we send an AI model your onboarding answers and the lines you've kept. We don't send your name or your email.
- **Daily reads, stories and images** are written once for everyone. They are not made from your data.

We ask AI providers not to keep what we send or use it to train their models. AI replies can be wrong and are not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice. Please don't share sensitive personal details with Milo.

## How we use your data

- To run your account and sync it across your phones.
- To show your habits, streak, widgets and daily lines.
- To answer your messages to Milo.
- To send the nudges and reminders you turn on. These are scheduled on your phone.
- To manage your subscription and free trial.
- To keep the app secure and fix problems.

We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

## Who processes data for us

- **Supabase** (servers in Ireland, EU): database, sign-in and sign-in emails.
- **Resend:** delivering sign-in emails.
- **OpenRouter** and the AI model providers it uses: Milo's replies and your daily lines.
- **RevenueCat:** checking your subscription with Apple and Google.
- **Apple and Google:** app distribution, payments and subscriptions.
- **Cloudflare:** this website and our support email.

Some of these providers process data outside the European Union. When they do, the transfer is protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Legal bases (GDPR)

- **Contract:** to provide the app and the subscription you signed up for.
- **Consent:** for notifications, which you can turn off at any time in Profile → Notifications or in your phone's settings.
- **Legitimate interest:** to keep the app secure and working.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database right away, and from our backups within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, and you can object to or limit how we use it.

- **Export:** in the app, Profile → My account → Export my data.
- **Delete:** in the app, Profile → My account → Delete my account, or see [dscpln.app/delete-account](/delete-account).
- **Anything else:** write to [support@dscpln.app](mailto:support@dscpln.app). We reply within 30 days.

You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority.

## Age

dscpln is for people aged 16 and over.

## The website

dscpln.app doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Cloudflare, which hosts it, keeps standard server logs to protect the site.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For important changes, we tell you in the app first.
