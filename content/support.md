# Support

We usually reply within 2 working days.

Email us at [**support@dscpln.app**](mailto:support@dscpln.app), or in the app from **Profile → Help & support → Email us**. Tell us your phone model and what happened.

## Signing in

### I don't get my sign-in code

Check your spam or promotions folder, and that you typed your email exactly. The code is 8 digits and works for a few minutes; tap **Resend** to get a new one. Still nothing? Write to us.

## Subscription

### How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with 3 days free, once per account. We remind you the day before it ends. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.

### How do I cancel?

In the app: **Profile → Manage subscription**. Or on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → dscpln. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

### I paid but the app still asks me to subscribe

Tap **Restore** at the bottom of the plans screen. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with. If that doesn't work, write to us with the date of the purchase.

### How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle payments, so refunds go through them: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) for iPhone, or Google Play → Order history for Android.

## Habits, nudges and widgets

### I don't get my nudges

Check that notifications for dscpln are on in your phone's settings, and set your nudge times in **Profile → Notifications**. Focus modes and Low Power Mode can hold notifications back.

### My widget doesn't update

Open dscpln once after ticking a habit, then go back to your Home Screen. If it still shows old numbers, remove the widget and add it again. You can change its style in **Profile → Widgets**.

### My streak broke but I did my habits

Streaks follow the time zone of your phone. If you travelled or changed the time, write to us and tell us the date.

## Milo

### Milo stopped answering

Milo has a limit of 30 messages a day. It resets the next day.

## Your data

### Where are my journal and audio?

Your journal, mood, notes and audio stay on your phone. If you delete the app or change phones, they don't come with you.

### How do I delete my account?

In the app: **Profile → My account → Delete my account**. Or see [Delete account](/delete-account).

## Need urgent help?

dscpln is not a crisis service. If you're struggling, contact local emergency services, use **Need to talk now?** in your profile, or find a free helpline at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).
